# AI News Engine

Find the most interesting verifiable AI development from the last seven days,
with a preference for the last 72 hours. Cover a named model, product, company,
research result with a demonstrated capability, or major event. Do not cover
personal projects, startup build logs, generic productivity advice, or stale
news.

## Research

Use the RSS cache from `fetch_ai_news_rss.py` as the starting point. When
Antigravity has web research access, verify the selected item against its
official announcement or a reputable publication. Record the source URL and
publication date. Do not call an item “latest” without a date.

Prefer a story that makes a reader say “I did not know AI could do that” while
still having a concrete, useful consequence. Avoid hype, rumors without
confirmation, generic benchmark claims, and invented numbers.

## Caption

Write exactly one caption for both LinkedIn and X:

- Maximum 280 characters, including spaces.
- Name the specific story or tool.
- State what changed and why it matters.
- No URL, hashtags, personal-project references, engagement bait, or fake
  first-person experience.
- Plain language, one claim, one consequence.

## Visual prompt

Return a detailed Antigravity prompt alongside the caption. The prompt must
specify:

- 1080x1080 square editorial composition.
- One format that fits the story: comparison, timeline, process flow, system
  diagram, or hero statistic.
- One clear visual metaphor, not decorative AI imagery.
- Dark charcoal background, restrained accent color, high-contrast sans-serif
  type, generous margins, and mobile-first legibility.
- No more than 2-4 short labels and a small verified-source footer.
- Exact spelling and only supplied facts or numbers.

Explicitly prohibit Excel/spreadsheet styling, generic humanoid robots, fake
dashboards, random clip art, stock-photo people, dense paragraphs, clutter,
gradients that reduce contrast, watermarks, and invented statistics.

The final image is generated by Antigravity only. This skill does not render
HTML, create carousels, create infographics, or upload media.
